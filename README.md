The Wonmor Open Source Fund is a planned way to support independent open-source builders in Uzbekistan, beyond the founder's own projects. This repository is where its rules, application template and reports will live once it opens.

Everything below describes what is planned. Nothing here has happened yet. The [sponsorship page on wonmor.io](https://wonmor.io/company/sponsorship/) is the source of truth; if the two ever differ, the page on wonmor.io applies.

## Why it is not open yet

Wonmor is a self-funded brand, not a company: there is no legal entity, no board and no investors, and it has taken no outside money. Today the money that keeps Wonmor's projects online comes from the founder's own income.

The fund depends on Wonmor first becoming a legal entity, which is also planned. Until then, no applications are accepted. When the fund opens, its criteria, application template and reports will be public, and this README will say so in its first line.

## What it will support

Independent open-source projects built in Uzbekistan, by people other than the founder. The kinds of support, and any amounts, will be published here before the fund opens.

## Criteria

These are the criteria Wonmor already uses to decide which of its own projects it supports. A project gets support when it meets most of them:

- **It is for people we know.** Someone real needs it, and we can ask them whether it works.
- **One person can keep it running.** The design has to be simple enough to operate, back up and restore without a team.
- **It is open where it can be.** Code is public by default; when it is not, the project says so.
- **It respects privacy and faith.** No tracking for its own sake, and no feature that works against the people using it.
- **It can be measured.** We can tell from numbers, not impressions, whether it is getting better.

The final criteria for the fund will be published here when it opens and may add to this list.

## How selection will work

The details are not decided yet and will be published here before the fund opens. What is planned:

1. **A public template.** Applications will use the template in this repository ([`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/application.yml`](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/application.yml)). It is a draft until the fund opens.
2. **The criteria above.** Each application will be read against the published criteria.
3. **A decision with a reason.** In line with the [charter](https://wonmor.io/charter/), decisions will be written down with the reason they were made.
4. **Reports.** Supported projects and what they received will appear in the yearly report.

## The yearly report (planned)

Wonmor plans a yearly report on what it supported. The first one will cover 2026 and will be published after the year ends. It will list each spending category, the projects each one served, and what changed during the year. Whether it includes exact amounts is still being decided. Either way, it will contain only real spending: nothing estimated and nothing rounded up to look bigger.

Once the fund opens, fund support will be part of that report.

## What we will not do

- **No promises before opening.** No applications are accepted and no money is promised until this README says the fund is open.
- **No equity and no revenue share.** Wonmor does not buy equity and does not take a share of revenue.
- **No invented numbers.** Reports contain only real spending, never estimates or rounded-up figures.
- **No quiet changes.** Wonmor is not a grant-maker, an accelerator or an investor, and has no sponsorship deals with outside companies. If any of that changes, the sponsorship page on wonmor.io changes first, and this repository follows.
